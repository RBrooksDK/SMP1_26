### How to use the practice exams

1. Download the PDF and solve the assignments under exam conditions: 3 hours for Part 1 on paper, followed by 1 hour for Part 2 in a Jupyter Notebook.
2. Each practice exam has a matching flow in Wiseflow. Enter your answers there, using the same numbering as in the PDF (Assignment 1.1, 1.2, …).
3. Use **Check answer** in Wiseflow to see whether your answer is correct.

The answer fields follow the rules described on the [Exam](exam.md) page: non-negative integers, irreducible fractions, decimal values with the stated number of decimal places, and a pre-printed minus sign for negative answers.

For the practice exams you only need to enter your answers in Wiseflow. At the exam you must also hand in your documentation: one PDF file for Part 1 and one Jupyter Notebook for Part 2.
